# AgriCoop Marketplace — User Guide

Plain-language instructions for each role. The marketplace is a cooperative-based platform for buying and selling Philippine agricultural products (primarily Agusan del Sur / Caraga region), with face-to-face (F2F) and online payment options.

---

## For Everyone

### Creating an account

1. Go to the homepage and click **Sign in** (top right).
2. Choose **Register** and enter your name, email, and password.
3. Verify your email using the link sent to your inbox (if email verification is enabled for your environment).
4. Sign in. New accounts start as **buyers**.

### Signing in and out

- **Sign in:** click **Sign in**, enter email and password, submit.
- **Sign out:** open your profile menu (top-right avatar/name) and choose **Sign out**.
- If your session expires, you will be returned to the sign-in page. Sign in again to continue.

### Using the mobile menu

- On phones and small tablets, tap the **menu button** (☰) in the header to open navigation.
- Tap any item to navigate — the menu closes automatically.
- Tap outside the menu or press **Escape** to close it.

## Buyer

You can browse products, build a cart, place orders, pay (online transfer with proof, or cash on delivery/face-to-face), and message sellers.

### Browse and search products

1. Open **Marketplace** from the navigation.
2. Use the **search box** to find products by name.
3. Use **category** and **location/cooperative** filters to narrow results.
4. Tap a product card to see details: price, stock, images, seller/cooperative, and fulfillment type (ready stock vs. made-to-order).
5. Use the heart icon to save a product to favorites (if shown).

### Add to cart and checkout

1. On a product page, set the quantity and tap **Add to Cart**.
2. Open **Cart** to review items. Change quantities or remove items.
3. Tap **Checkout**. First choose **Fulfillment**: **Pickup** (you collect the order) or **Delivery** (the order is delivered to your address). **Delivery requires a combined order weight of at least 20 kg** — below that, Delivery is disabled with an explanation showing your current weight and how much more you need; Pickup always remains available.
4. Then choose a compatible **payment method**:
   - For **Pickup**: **Online Payment** or **Cash on Pickup**.
   - For **Delivery**: **Online Payment** or **Cash on Delivery**.
   - **Online Payment** — the AgriCoop QR code is shown with the payee name/number and amount. After paying in your wallet app, upload a photo/screenshot of the receipt; a cooperative officer verifies it before fulfillment. Works for both pickup and delivery.
   - **Cash on Pickup / Cash on Delivery** — pay in cash when you collect or receive the order; an officer confirms cash received.
5. If you chose **Delivery**, fill in the delivery details (recipient name, contact number, province, city, barangay, street address). Pickup orders do not need an address.
6. Submit the order. You will see an **order confirmation** and can track it under **Orders/Activity** — each order shows its fulfillment method (Pickup/Delivery) and payment method separately.

### Track orders and communicate

- **Activity/Orders** shows each order's status (pending approval, confirmed, preparing, ready for pickup/delivery, completed, cancelled).
- Your dashboard's **Online payments** section lists online orders awaiting verification, verified payments, and rejected proofs — upload or resubmit receipts there.
- Tap an order to see details and timeline.
- Use **Messages** to chat with the seller or cooperative about your order.

### Get help

- **Support** lets you open a ticket for questions or problems. You can reply in the thread until it is resolved.

## Farmer / Seller

You sell your produce through the cooperative. You manage your listings, confirm orders, arrange payment, and chat with buyers.

### List a product

1. Open **Dashboard → My Listings** (or **Products**).
2. Tap **Add Product / New Listing**.
3. Fill in: product name, category, price, stock quantity, unit, description, images, and fulfillment type (on-hand stock vs. made-to-order/pre-order).
4. Submit. The cooperative admin may review or moderate listings depending on platform settings.
5. You can edit, update stock, archive, or delete your own listings later.

### Manage orders

1. New orders appear in your dashboard and under **Orders**.
2. For each order you can **accept**, **reject**, or **mark ready** depending on the order stage.
3. Confirm availability when asked (especially for made-to-order items).
4. Coordinate payment: buyers pay online (GCash/Maya/etc.) and upload proof, or pay cash on pickup/delivery.
5. Update fulfillment status so buyers see progress.

### Messaging and support

- Use **Messages** to talk with buyers about orders.
- Use **Support** for platform issues.

## OfficerAdmin (Cooperative Admin)

You administer one cooperative: its members, listings, orders, payments, and payment options.

### Applying to become a Cooperative Admin

1. Go to **Sign in → Register as Cooperative Admin** (or visit **Applications → Cooperative Admin**).
2. Select your **cooperative** from the list of active cooperatives.
3. Upload a clear photo/PDF of a **government-issued ID** (private — only SuperAdmin reviewers can view it).
4. Submit the application. Status becomes **Pending**.
5. You cannot access OfficerAdmin features until a SuperAdmin approves you. You will see a pending/rejected notice instead.

### After approval

1. Sign in — your dashboard is scoped to **your cooperative only**.
2. **Members/Users:** view buyers and sellers in your cooperative; you can manage (edit, suspend, or permanently delete eligible accounts). Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.
3. **Listings:** review and moderate products listed by your members.
4. **Orders:** approve or reject incoming orders; verify payment proofs for online payments.
5. **Payment Options:** configure which payment methods (GCash, Maya, etc.) your cooperative accepts and upload/rotate QR codes.
6. **Reports:** view cooperative-level sales and order reports; export CSV.
7. **Support:** handle support tickets for your members.

> You only see data belonging to your cooperative. You cannot see or affect other cooperatives.

## SuperAdmin

You administer the entire platform.

### Cooperative Admin applications

1. Open **Dashboard → Applications** (or the applications panel).
2. Review each pending application: applicant name, cooperative, and ID document.
3. **Approve** — the applicant becomes an OfficerAdmin of that cooperative and can sign in.
4. **Reject** — the applicant is notified and cannot access OfficerAdmin features.

### Platform management

- **Cooperatives:** create, edit, suspend, or manage all cooperatives.
- **Users:** view all accounts; manage roles; suspend or permanently delete accounts (permanent — cannot be undone; requires typing confirmation).
- **Listings/Orders:** platform-wide moderation and oversight.
- **Payment options:** manage system-level payment configuration.
- **Reports & Audit Logs:** platform-wide reports and audit trail.

### Permanent account deletion

- Open the user's row → **Delete Account**.
- Read the warning, then confirm. Personal data, documents, and login are permanently removed.
- Historical orders/transactions are retained but anonymized (the user reference becomes blank) so financial records stay intact.

## Troubleshooting

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| Can't sign in | Check email/password; reset password via **Forgot password**; verify email if required. |
| Page shows endless loading | Refresh; check your connection; sign out and back in if the session expired. |
| "Permission denied" | Your role may not allow that action, or your account is pending/suspended. Contact your cooperative admin or support. |
| Payment proof upload fails | Use JPG/PNG/PDF under the size limit; check your connection and retry. |
| Order stuck | Message the seller/cooperative or open a **Support** ticket. |
